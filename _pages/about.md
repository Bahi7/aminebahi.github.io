I am a PhD researcher in cryptography with the CASCADE team at ENS Paris and a member of the Encrypted Systems Lab at Brown University.
My research interests include structured encryption, PIR/ORAM, volume-hiding systems, and Bayesian leakage analysis.

I am also the Co-founder & CTO at Rabiata.
I am open to research collaborations, consulting, and technical discussions in applied cryptography and privacy-preserving systems.

This site contains my publications, selected projects, and professional links.
