This page tracks research work connected to privacy-preserving systems and secure data processing.

It includes collaboration contexts linked to the CASCADE team (ENS Paris) and the Encrypted Systems Lab at Brown University, with emphasis on deployable cryptographic techniques.

Representative themes:

- encrypted data structures
- secure query processing
- practical privacy in distributed systems
